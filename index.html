About Me

AI Engineer with 2+ years of professional experience designing, fine-tuning, and deploying large-scale AI systems in production. I specialize in Agentic AI, Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), and LLM Fine-Tuning, with hands-on expertise in building multi-agent workflows, fine-tuning open-source models, and shipping production-ready AI microservices.
Track Record: At Erly Studios (06/2025 – 07/2026), I delivered 13+ production AI products serving 500–1,500 users across healthcare, venture capital, compliance, travel, research, and workforce automation — architecting 30+ autonomous LangGraph agents and 11 FastAPI microservices with 150+ REST endpoints, and achieving 60–95% efficiency gains across all systems.

Published Researcher: IEEE-published research on multilingual road traffic sign detection and OCR. Currently completing Bachelor's degree in Artificial Intelligence while maintaining full-time engineering role.
